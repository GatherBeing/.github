## Get early access

The full Gather Being app is in **closed testing** ahead of public launch.

- **iOS** — the app on the App Store is our waitlist app: download it to register
  your interest and we will let you know the moment the full platform opens.
- **Android** — the app is in closed testing and will reach Google Play at launch.
  Join the list at [gatherbeing.com](https://gatherbeing.com) to be told when it does.

Already testing with us? You will get the full app through TestFlight or the Play
internal track — not through the public listings above.

## Engineering

This organisation holds the code behind the platform. The repositories are private;
this page is the public face of the team that builds them.

We run a UK investment platform end to end: a Postgres core with a transactional
ledger, edge functions, a staff admin surface, and native iOS and Android clients
built with Capacitor from one TypeScript codebase. Everything ships by pull request
against protected branches, with contract tests over the financial schema as a
required check.

**We are not currently hiring.** For anything else, [support@gatherbeing.com](mailto:support@gatherbeing.com).
